Read our full mission

At GrabWallet, our mission is to revolutionize fundraising through the power of fanraising, transforming everyday digital interactions into meaningful financial opportunities. Through GrabWallet and Grab TV, we connect fans, athletes, schools, sports teams, clubs, businesses, and communities through an innovative digital ecosystem designed to turn engagement into ongoing financial support.

We believe fundraising should go beyond traditional campaigns, seasonal events, and one-time contributions. We believe in the power of fans—their passion, loyalty, participation, and influence—to create lasting opportunities for the people, teams, and organizations they support.

Our goal is to build a platform where fanraising becomes part of everyday life, empowering supporters to make a difference through everyday engagement and creating opportunities to generate funds 365 days a year.

At GrabWallet, every fan matters, every tap creates opportunity, and every day is a chance to make an impact.

Before you sign with anyone

Five questions to ask any fundraising platform.

01What do you collect from our students' phones?+
GrabWallet answer

Nothing. Supporters opt in on their own. Your program shares a QR code at games, in a newsletter or on a flyer, and anyone who wants to join scans it and enters their own name and phone number. No student contact lists, rosters or pupil records ever change hands.

02What happens when the campaign ends?+
GrabWallet answer

There is no campaign to end. The program keeps earning as long as supporters use the card at reward partners.

03Do our supporters get charged every month?+
GrabWallet answer

No. There is no donation or charge to use GrabWallet. Supporters may earn rewards on purchases they routinely make.

04What percentage do you keep?+
GrabWallet answer

The split between your program, your supporters and GrabWallet is written into your program's agreement, so you see it before you sign.

05When do we get paid?+
GrabWallet answer

Payouts go to your program's own account on the schedule written into your agreement.
